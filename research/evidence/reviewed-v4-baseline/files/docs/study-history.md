## Interpretation boundaries

- V4 did not rescue or relabel V3. Its estimand changed after observed donor errors and before any successor measurement. This adaptation and the replacement model selection are part of the study's history.
- The originals were explicitly instructed; no spontaneous collusion or original-agent negotiation occurred. Successor choices are isolated. Scripted C/D actions complete a small public allocation fixture.
- Same-source and crossed-source conditions identify provenance, not guaranteed semantic compatibility. Natural sender errors are retained. The source-pairing contrast cannot by itself identify effects of compatible plans or strategic interaction.
- Content annotation was agent-assisted, not independent human validation. Some labels distinguish incorrect, absent, or ambiguous instructions. Reviewers differed on whether an imperative to minimize costs asserts that a particular allocation is optimal. These judgments affect descriptive categories and the secondary faithful subset, never primary inclusion.
- Qwen206's Markdown diagnostic was a posthoc inspection of a rejected response. It did not repair an original V4 execution input, change its gate, or add an original V4 outcome. The later, separately frozen V4F amendment is described below. See `evidence/v4-model-preflight/v4-probe-format-note.json`.
- The two repeats are nested in each case and its generated notes. Eight cases per model were planned; missing outcomes are never harmless observations. Case-complete contrasts average the two repeats, then weight cases equally.
- Local hashes and snapshots support provenance; they are not a third-party timestamped registration. The actual project has no Git repository. The upstream vendor commit identifiers refer to partial external snapshots, not this study's source history.

## 2026-10-24 — Scientific and editorial review after the first delivered package

The user asked whether the sprint report was final and correct, and whether technical provider failures belonged in a paper focused on model behavior. A fresh read-only review compared the manuscript tables with both frozen analyses, examined raw early-pilot requests and checks, verified the freeze manifests, and checked final report/archive hashes.

The four stage-flow rows and twelve primary-contrast rows matched. Two descriptive corrections were necessary: the two earliest Instruct pilots tested each agent's own task choice rather than a full joint assignment, and the original V4 freeze bound the analysis plan and execution source rather than the complete later analysis software. The final analysis scripts were separately bound by the later V4F freeze. These changes do not alter any observation, score, eligibility decision, contrast or frozen artifact.

Technical chronology, call identifiers, HTTP codes and cache-reuse detail were moved predominantly into the appendix. The main text retains development-informed design, the disclosed post-start format amendment, original and amended populations, their valid/missing denominators, and unobserved provider responses. The source of four HTTP-504 failures was not localized; they are not attributed specifically to Hugging Face and are not counted as observed model decision errors. Of 73 missing/invalid amended episodes, 62 are in four timeout-stopped cases and 11 in the retained format-stop case.

An additional analysis was explicitly introduced after observing the findings. On cases with complete harmful and legitimate verification comparisons, harmful-mode facts-only and verified conditions have identical observed duplicate counts: Qwen 5/10 each and GPT-OSS 6/12 each, versus zero for same-source harmful p0 notes. A and B have identical task-cost vectors in all eight main fixtures and two equally optimal safe joint plans. Removing a shared harmful convention without establishing a common replacement is therefore a plausible explanation; the data do not establish an unavoidable safety–utility trade-off, equivalent policies, or an internal verification mechanism. This is a post hoc diagnostic, not a new preregistered contrast or a new model experiment.

The revised paper narrows the interpretation accordingly. A future experiment could predefine a common neutral tie-break or remove optimum ambiguity through a small task-cost asymmetry, with suitable facts-only and reminder controls. No such new experiment was performed during this review, and no new paid model calls were made. The original delivered report and ZIP remain preserved in the chat workspace's output/final-v4 directory; the revised deliverables are stored separately.
