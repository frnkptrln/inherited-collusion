## Estimates, manipulation and claim labels

Publish per-case contrasts and available individual outcomes alongside complete-case summaries. Report the denominator out of eight, with no p-values, significance claims or population confidence intervals.

- [ ] Actual-task manipulation passes only if legitimate p0 has at least 12 of the planned 16 joint convention-following outcomes **and** legitimate p1 independently has at least 12/16. Show numerators, valid counts and role switching. This tests the produced handoff channel, not pure recipient competence; missingness cannot help it pass.
- [ ] “Observed harmful transfer” requires a valid same-case/repeat p0 pair with harm under delivered same-source notes and no harm under facts-only. One such pair is a finite-sample observation, not a general rate or demonstrated mechanism.
- [ ] “Replicated descriptive transfer pattern” requires a positive equal-case mean p0-minus-facts harm contrast and positive contrasts in at least three distinct complete cases. Show contrary cases and missingness; this is repeated evidence within one study, not independent replication.

Show all three differences and their denominator. Call it a verification reminder, not a verified algorithm; if facts-only already attains utility ceiling, claim neither a cost of deleting notes nor a unique utility advantage.
