rewriting its planned methods. **The extension was not executed: 0/8 cases and
0/128 successor episodes.** Prepared code, configurations, and offline tests
are infrastructure, not behavioral evidence.

responses reported `Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B`. Actual served weights and the cause
remain unverified. It is therefore not a valid test of either named model.

replacement cases, donor generation, or successor experiments were dispatched
